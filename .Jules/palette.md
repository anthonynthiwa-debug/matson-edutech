
## 2025-05-13 - [IconButton Tooltip Integration]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons often lack immediate context for users. Integrating tooltips directly into the  primitive ensures consistent accessibility across the app without requiring developers to manually wrap each button in a tooltip component.
**Action:** Always prefer the `tooltip` prop on `IconButton` instead of the native `title` attribute to provide a better, themed UX.
